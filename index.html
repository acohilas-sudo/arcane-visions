Commission Sacred Work

Built to the Order's Specifications
✦ ✦ ✦

Arcane Visions accepts a limited number of private commissions each year. Each piece begins with a single conversation about the order, the symbolic source material, the intended use, and the timeline.

Commissions are accepted from lodges, chapters, working groups, and individual collectors. Bas-relief work, devotional paintings, ceremonial portraiture, heraldic art, and symbolic objects have all been produced to specification.

Production is deliberate. The atelier does not work at scale. Materials are chosen for ceremonial life and for the long horizon of a collector's archive — not for visual effect alone.

On Commissioning

Each commission is priced to its scope — the materials, the scale, and the complexity of the work. Share what you envision, and the first response will include an honest estimate alongside the material proposal and timeline.

The Acquisition Process

  1. I
    Inquiry

    You share the work you envision — its symbolism, scale, and intended use.

  2. II
    Consultation

    We respond personally to discuss scope, materials, provenance, and fit.

  3. III
    Design Planning

    Source diagrams and a material proposal are prepared and confirmed with you.

  4. IV
    Craftsmanship

    The piece is built by hand, deliberately, with progress shared along the way.

  5. V
    Delivery

    The finished work is documented, carefully prepared, and shipped worldwide.

Inquiries receive a personal response within 24–48 hours to begin the consultation.
