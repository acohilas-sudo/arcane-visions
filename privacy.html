Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2026

Arcane Visions ("we," "our," or "us") respects the privacy of everyone who visits arcanevisions.art and corresponds with the atelier. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to it.

1. What We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you write to begin a commission, inquire about a piece, or respond to correspondence we have begun with you. This includes:

  • Name and email address
  • The content of your inquiry, commission brief, or correspondence
  • Shipping address and any contact details you choose to provide if a delivery is arranged

We also receive standard technical information from your browser when you visit the site (browser type, IP address, pages visited, referring URL). This is used in aggregate to understand how the site is reached and where it should be improved.

2. How We Use It

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, to discuss and confirm a commission or acquisition, to arrange delivery, and to maintain a private record of correspondence with collectors and practitioners we have worked with.

3. What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not maintain a public mailing list. We do not publish the names of collectors or commissioning bodies without explicit consent.

4. Third-Party Services

The contact and inquiry forms on this site are processed by a third-party form service (Formspree) that relays your message to us by email. The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages, which provides standard infrastructure analytics. No transactional or payment data is processed on this site.

5. Your Rights

You may request that we delete the record of your correspondence at any time, or request a copy of the information we hold about you, by writing to info@arcanevisionsstudios.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is materially changed, the revised version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
