heading: "Built to the Order's Specifications"
body: |-
  Arcane Visions accepts a limited number of private commissions each year. Each piece begins with a single conversation about the order, the symbolic source material, the intended use, and the timeline.

  Commissions are accepted from lodges, chapters, working groups, and individual collectors. Regalia, altar furnishings, bas-relief work, devotional objects, and one-of-a-kind symbolic art have all been produced to specification.

  Production is deliberate. The atelier does not work at scale. Materials are chosen for ceremonial life and for the long horizon of a collector's archive — not for visual effect alone.

  Pricing reflects the time, material quality, and symbolic precision involved in each piece. Where a commission requires unusual research, original design, or museum-grade construction, scope and price are agreed in writing before work begins.
