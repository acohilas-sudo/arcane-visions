heading: "Bespoke Esoteric Commissions"
body: |-
  Arcane Visions accepts a limited number of private commissions each year. Each piece begins with a single conversation about the subject, the symbolic source material, the intended placement, and the timeline.

  Commissions are accepted from collectors, patrons, and institutions. Bas-relief works, devotional paintings, portraiture, and one-of-a-kind esoteric art have all been created to the collector's brief.

  Production is deliberate. The atelier does not work at scale. Materials are chosen for permanence and for the long horizon of a collector's archive — not for visual effect alone.

  Pricing reflects the time, material quality, and symbolic precision involved in each piece. Where a commission requires unusual research, original design, or museum-grade construction, scope and price are agreed in writing before work begins.
